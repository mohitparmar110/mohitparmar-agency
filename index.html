How I work

Find the leak

I look at the offer, journey, creative, traffic quality and store behaviour before proposing a redesign or another campaign.

02

03

Measure what changed

Good to know

Before we work together.

What kind of work are you best suited for?

Projects where creative, e-commerce and growth overlap — for example a Shopify store that needs better product presentation and conversion, a brand that needs a stronger paid creative system, or a team that needs one senior operator across both design and growth.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I’m based in Mumbai and can work with teams in India, the UK, UAE and other markets remotely. The exact setup can be project-based, retained or role-based.

Can you audit an existing website before a rebuild?

Yes. In most cases I prefer that. A full redesign is not automatically the answer; the audit should identify whether the real issue is positioning, UX, merchandising, traffic, creative, tracking or technical execution.
